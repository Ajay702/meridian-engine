# Service Level Agreement

## Uptime Commitment
The platform API is guaranteed to be available 99.95 percent of each calendar month for Business and Enterprise plans. The Starter plan carries a 99.5 percent uptime target with no service credits.

## Service Credits
If monthly uptime falls below 99.95 percent, Business customers receive a 10 percent service credit. Below 99.0 percent the credit rises to 25 percent. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the incident through the support portal.

## Exclusions
Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance does not count as downtime. Outages caused by customer misconfiguration are excluded.
