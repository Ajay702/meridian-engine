# Data Retention

## Active Accounts
Application logs are retained for 30 days. Audit logs are retained for 1 year and can be exported to customer storage at any time.

## Account Deletion
When an account is closed, customer data is soft-deleted immediately and permanently purged after 30 days. Backups containing the data expire within 35 days. Customers may request immediate purge through their account manager.

## Legal Hold
Data under legal hold is exempt from automatic deletion until the hold is released by the legal team.
