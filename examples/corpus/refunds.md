# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Customers on monthly plans can request a full refund within 14 days of the first charge. Annual plans are eligible for a prorated refund within 30 days of renewal. Usage-based overage charges are non-refundable.

## Processing
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Card refunds take 5 to 10 business days to appear on the statement. Bank transfer refunds take up to 15 business days.

## Exceptions
Accounts suspended for abuse or terms-of-service violations are not eligible for refunds. Enterprise contracts follow the refund terms in their signed order form.
