# Customer Onboarding

## Workspace Setup
New workspaces are provisioned automatically within 2 minutes of signup. The first administrator can invite up to 5 teammates on the Starter plan and unlimited teammates on Business.

## API Keys
API keys are created from the developer settings page. Each key can be scoped to read-only or read-write access and should be rotated at least every 180 days.

## Support Channels
Starter customers get email support with a 2 business-day response target. Business customers get chat support with a 4 hour response target, and Enterprise customers have a dedicated technical account manager.
